Kawaii — 1 Player vs AI (single HTML)

You are Blue. AI is Red. Click a blue piece, then click a highlighted destination.
Rules (this implementation)
  • Move 1 piece per turn to an adjacent empty node (follow the lines).
  • No captures, no jumps.
  • You win if the opponent has no legal moves.
  • Optional variant: Center = instant win (toggle above).
Tips:
  • The center is a mobility hub — strong, but don’t get boxed in.
  • Try to reduce the AI’s mobility (number of legal moves).
  • Undo rolls back one full turn (you + AI).
