Rules (digital version):
• Two players alternate turns. On your turn you may pick up one puck by clicking it (only if it’s nearly resting).
• If you trigger the trap (gate moves) during your turn, your turn ends immediately: next player.
• Press Space (or button) to release the spring gate. The slam pushes pucks across the divider to the right side.
• Goal: just collect most pucks (simple scoring).

Tip: Let the pucks settle before picking, or you might “cause movement” and lose your turn.
